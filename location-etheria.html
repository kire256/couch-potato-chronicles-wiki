Etheria

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Locations

The game-logic world where the entire story takes place. It operates on RPG mechanics — levels, save points, respawning, spell programming, loot drops, XP, and job boards are all fundamental laws of nature rather than abstractions. Etheria is a large continent or world featuring multiple kingdoms (Questgivria, Zhakara), diverse biomes (forests, mountains, bogs, coastlines), and populated by elves, humans, dwarves, slimes, fairies, dragons, cat-people, spiderfolk, and other races. The world is threatened by an ancient countdown tied to Entropy’s bargain with the first Questgiver king. Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos, Couch Potato Crisis
