Slimewater / Slime's Row

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Locations

A settlement in the Slime Federation, home to intelligent slime citizens. The Belly Acres farm is nearby. Tasha and Kiwi pass through here during the Chicken Chaser side quest. Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos

Location details

  • Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos
  • Aliases: Slime Federation, Slimewater
  • Description: A settlement in the Slime Federation, home to intelligent slime citizens. The Belly Acres farm is nearby. Tasha and Kiwi pass through here during the Chicken Chaser side quest.
  • Image Generation Prompt: A quirky fantasy frontier town at the edge of slime territory, viewed from outside the city gates at midday. The architecture is a blend of human and slime aesthetics — squat, rounded buildings with thick gel-coated walls that glisten faintly in shades of translucent blue, green, and amber, as though the structures themselves are alive. Roofs are smooth and organic, lacking sharp angles, with chimney-like protrusions that vent gentle wisps of steam. The main street is wide and paved in smooth stone, bustling with gelatinous slime citizens of various sizes and colours — teal, purple, lime, and amber — their semi-transparent bodies wobbling as they move. A few humans and elves walk among them. To one side stands a post office with a carved wooden sign depicting a swallow in flight; through its window, a bored clerk can be seen with a novel open on his counter. Near the eastern gate, a covered wagon is parked alongside riding chickens pecking at the ground. A distant train station platform is visible at the far end of town, its gnome-ticketed booth and dwarven-made rails catching the light. The surrounding landscape is rolling green grassland and farmland — chicken coops and vegetable plots visible beyond the town walls. Warm, bright afternoon light. The mood is peaceful and faintly absurd — a functioning town where sentient blobs go about their daily business alongside taller races. Fantasy RPG art style, detailed and colourful, with a light-hearted tone that belies the dangerous wilderness beyond.
