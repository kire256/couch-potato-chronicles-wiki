The Selkie's Tear

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Locations

A tavern in Aldrige, marked by a wooden signpost with “The Selkie’s Tear” engraved into it. The interior is large, with dozens of round tables. The patrons are all human, while the servers are all elven — reflecting the town’s slave hierarchy. The bartender is a bald-headed man in a plain white shirt. The tavern serves as the contact point for Caymie Jakobson’s job posting about escaped slave-elves. Appears in: Couch Potato Crisis
