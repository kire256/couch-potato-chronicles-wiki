Zhakaran anti-mastery law

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Concepts & Lore

Zhakara’s foundational law forbids one human from claiming mastery over another, and later versions of the law are expanded into nation-scale magical enforcement. Appears in: Couch Potato Crisis
