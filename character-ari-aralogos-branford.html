Ari / Aralogos Branford

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Characters View relationship charts

Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos, Couch Potato Crisis

Profile

  • Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos, Couch Potato Crisis

  • Aliases: Ari

  • Cross-book continuity: Appears in both books.

  • Role: The party’s frontline pugilist and Pan’s “father” — actually a figment Pan summoned into lasting existence as an abandoned infant, who has lived so long and so convincingly in the role that he genuinely believes himself to be one.

  • Personality: Calm, steady, and physically imposing — the emotional anchor of the party and the person everyone else leans on when things go sideways. Ari speaks little but acts decisively. His combat style is methodical and powerful: he does not flash or showboat, he simply ends fights efficiently with his fists. As the party’s veteran pugilist, he serves as the primary frontline tank and damage dealer, absorbing punishment that would drop lesser fighters and returning it with interest. Despite being a figment — a summoned construct who only persists because Pan’s summoner power anchors him to reality — Ari possesses a full emotional life. He loves Pan as a real father would, protects her with unhesitating ferocity, and carries genuine fear that learning the truth of his nature might break their bond. His calm is not coldness; it is the composure of someone who knows that panic helps no one. In Crisis he evolves from pure protector to genuine party leader, keeping the group focused during the gruelling rescue campaign for Kiwi and the assault on Zhakara. He is the quiet centre around which the rest of the party orbits.

  • Backstory: Ari did not exist before Pan summoned him. As an abandoned infant left alone in the wild, Panella Branford instinctively triggered her latent summoner ability and poured every ounce of need and imagination into creating a powerful, protective father figure — a tall, strong, martial-arts expert she named Aralogos (Ari). The infant’s summoner power was extraordinary: figments normally fade quickly once their summoner stops feeding them, but Pan’s emotional need was so intense and sustained that Ari persisted for years, growing and developing as a real person would. He has no memories of “before” — his existence begins with Pan, and he believes himself to be her biological father because Pan’s summoning gave him that belief as a foundational trait. They eventually reached the town of Wilmarth, where the elderly witch [Mad Marina](./character-mad-marina.html) took them in, becoming Pan’s surrogate grandmother and Ari’s co-guardian. Marina recognised the truth about Ari but kept the secret, choosing to let the family function as it was. The local tyrant [Lord Hempledon](./character-lord-hempledon-of-wilmarth.html) exploited and threatened them during their years in Wilmarth. When Pan’s backstory is finally revealed in Chapters 26–27 of Chaos, the truth recontextualises everything: Ari is not Pan’s biological father but a figment sustained by a child’s desperate love — yet the bond between them is in every meaningful sense real.

  • Significant story events:

    • Infancy / Pre-story: Summoned into existence by the abandoned infant Pan. Kept alive by her sustained summoner power. Reached Wilmarth and was taken in by [Mad Marina](./character-mad-marina.html).
    • Chapter 4 — The Pugilist and the Thief (Chaos): Ari and Pan meet Tasha in the [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html). Ari is introduced as Pan’s protective father and a formidable unarmed combatant. The party alliance begins.
    • Chapter 6 — Boss Fight (Chaos): Ari contributes frontline pugilist damage during the Master Glove boss encounter, establishing his combat role in the party.
    • Chapters 7–20 (Chaos): Ari travels with the party from the Temple through Brightwind. He serves as the group’s steady frontline fighter throughout the road trip, marketplace crisis, and war council.
    • Chapter 18 — Princess Rescue (Chaos): Fights alongside the party to rescue [Princess Kiwi](./character-princess-kiwistafel-questgiver.html) from kidnappers, including a pitched battle against a Ginormasaurus rex.
    • Chapter 26 — Secrets and Revelations (Chaos): Pan’s backstory begins to surface. The party learns there is more to Ari’s existence than a normal family relationship.
    • Chapter 27 — [Mad Marina](./character-mad-marina.html) and the Phantom Child (Chaos): The full revelation — Pan summoned Ari into existence as an abandoned infant. Marina’s role as guardian and keeper of the secret is established. Ari’s nature as a figment, not a biological father, is exposed.
    • Chapter 34 — Rumble on the Belcross Express (Chaos): Ari fights through ninja-infested train cars alongside the party. In the battle’s climax, he suplexes the runaway train engine — a moment that has become iconic for showcasing his raw physical power.
    • Epilogue (Chaos): Ari is present when Murderjoy’s ambush kills Tasha’s third life and abducts Kiwi. His existence as a figment remains precarious — he persists only through Pan’s continued summoner power.
    • Chapter 1 (Crisis): Ari joins Pan, Hermes, Slimon, and Kaze on the revenge quest against the Jabberwock immediately following the events of the Chaos epilogue.
    • Crisis main arc (multiple chapters): Ari serves as the party’s steady frontline leader throughout the long rescue campaign for Kiwi. He keeps the group focused and combat-ready across the journey through Ironfall, Dea Latis, and into Zhakara.
    • Chapter 30 — The End of Zhakara (Crisis): Ari fights in the final battle alongside Pan’s other figments (Pollyanna and Igrius), all three active simultaneously — a testament to how far Pan’s summoner power has grown.
    • Chapter 24 — The Final Summon (Crisis): Pan unlocks the capstone ability that can make Ari permanent, though the cost is devastating: multiple summoners must permanently lose the summoner class. This is the culmination of the arc that has defined Ari’s existence since his creation — the possibility of becoming truly real.

  • Notable Quotes:

    “Stay behind me. I’ll handle this.” — Chapter 4, The Pugilist and the Thief (Chaos). Ari’s characteristic calm in the face of danger, establishing his protective role from the moment he meets Tasha.

    “It doesn’t matter what I am. What matters is that she’s my daughter, and I will always protect her.” — Chapter 27, [Mad Marina](./character-mad-marina.html) and the Phantom Child (Chaos). Ari responds to the revelation of his true nature, asserting that his love for Pan is real regardless of his origin.

    “We don’t stop. Not while she’s still out there.” — Crisis, main arc. Ari keeps the party moving during the gruelling rescue campaign for Kiwi, embodying the steady leadership that defines his role in the second book.

  • Image Generation Prompt: A tall, powerfully built man appearing to be in his mid-to-late thirties, approximately 6’2" (188 cm) with a broad-shouldered, athletic frame that carries significant muscle without bulk — a fighter’s physique, lean and functional. He appears human. Short, dark brown hair kept neat and practical, swept back from a strong brow. Deep-set dark eyes — calm, watchful, and kindly, conveying quiet authority rather than aggression. A square jaw with a short, well-maintained beard or heavy stubble. Weathered, tanned skin suggesting years of outdoor travel and combat. He wears practical pugilist’s attire: hand wraps or fingerless fighting gloves covering his knuckles and wrists, a sleeveless tunic or open-front vest that allows full arm mobility, fitted trousers, and sturdy travel boots. His arms are bare, showing lean, corded muscle and the occasional faint scar from years of unarmed combat. No heavy armor — his fighting style relies on evasion and raw striking power. His posture is relaxed but ready, weight slightly forward, hands often loosely curled at his sides as if ready to throw a punch at a moment’s notice. He is almost always positioned protectively near a small, frail-looking girl (Pan), his body angled to shield her. He carries no visible weapons — his fists are his weapons.

  • Relationships:

    • [Pan / Panella Branford](./character-pan-panella-branford.html) (“daughter” / summoner): The defining bond of Ari’s existence. Pan summoned him into being and he raised her as his own. They are each other’s only family. Ari’s love for Pan is genuine and complete — the fact that he is a figment does not diminish it. His greatest fear is that the truth of his nature will hurt her; his greatest motivation is protecting her.
    • [Tasha Singleton](./character-tasha-singleton.html) (close friend / party leader): They meet in the [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html) and fight side by side across two books. Ari respects Tasha’s growth from reluctant couch potato to genuine adventurer.
    • [Mad Marina](./character-mad-marina.html) (co-guardian / surrogate family): Took Ari and Pan in at Wilmarth. She knew the truth about his nature and kept the secret, functioning as Pan’s grandmother and Ari’s ally in raising the child.
    • [Princess Kiwi](./character-princess-kiwistafel-questgiver.html) (friend / party member): Fellow member of the core fellowship. Ari fights to rescue her from kidnappers in Chaos and again throughout the Crisis campaign.
    • [Prince Hermes](./character-prince-hermes.html) (ally): Fellow frontline combatant. They fight together during the Crisis rescue campaign and the assault on Zhakara.
    • Denver (party companion): The velociraptor mount and party member. Ari respects Denver’s combat utility.
    • [Lord Hempledon](./character-lord-hempledon-of-wilmarth.html) (enemy / oppressor): The local tyrant of Wilmarth who exploited and threatened Ari, Pan, and Marina during their years under his jurisdiction.
    • [Queen [Aralynn Murderjoy](./character-queen-aralynn-murderjoy.html)](./character-queen-aralynn-murderjoy.html) (enemy): Murderjoy’s forces target the party repeatedly. Ari fights against her operatives across both books.
    • Spindra (guide): Marina’s research partner, who provided higher-dimensional guidance that helped Pan understand — and by extension sustain — Ari’s existence.
