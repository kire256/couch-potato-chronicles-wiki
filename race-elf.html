Long-lived people who dominate Questgivria and trace their survival politics back to the Questgiver line and Entropy’s bargain. Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos, Couch Potato Crisis

Race details

  • Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos, Couch Potato Crisis
  • Cross-book continuity: Appears in both books.
  • Description by book:
    • Couch Potato Chaos: Long-lived people who dominate Questgivria and trace their survival politics back to the Questgiver line and Entropy’s bargain.
    • Couch Potato Crisis: A long-lived people heavily victimized under Zhakaran slavery; Kiwi, Fin, and many captives belong to this group.
  • References: Couch Potato Chaos: Chapter 20 - [Brightwind Keep](./location-brightwind-keep.html), Chapter 7 - The Jester and the King | Couch Potato Crisis: Absconded Princess, Beachhead, Prologue
