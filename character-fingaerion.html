Fingaerion

Source: wiki.md

Appears in: Couch Potato Crisis

Profile

  • Appears in: Couch Potato Crisis
  • Aliases: Fin
  • Personality: Deeply protective of Mara, whom he treats as a younger sister despite having no biological relation – the wiki’s relationship index describes their bond as “chosen sibling.” Resourceful and pragmatic in crisis, Fin is willing to do morally difficult things to survive and protect others. He frames the repeated killing of his former slaver Marnie via save-point cage as “justice and level-recovery rather than revenge,” revealing both his practical ruthlessness and his need to rationalize violence. Though quiet and deliberate by nature, he demonstrates sharp tactical thinking and an aptitude for administration that grows throughout the story. His mathemagician class reflects a mind comfortable with numerical logic and system exploitation – tools he turns against the very structure that enslaved him.
  • Backstory: Born in the high-elven village of Elanweer, Fin was captured and enslaved by Zhakaran slavers at a young age. In Zhakara he was rented out for dangerous dungeon runs alongside other enslaved elves, treated as disposable labor by his captors. His human owners, Marnie and Caymie, used him and others as cannon fodder in dungeon boss fights. During one such run (the Prologue dungeon), Marnie and Caymie turned on each other over the dungeon’s reward scroll; in the ensuing bloodbath, Fin claimed the dropped Reverse Stat Shuffle scroll – a rare classless ability. He and Mara escaped together into the Zhakaran wilderness, beginning their fugitive arc.
  • Significant story events:
    • Prologue (Crisis): Enslaved and rented out for a dungeon run. Witnesses Marnie and Caymie’s double betrayal. Claims the Reverse Stat Shuffle scroll from the bloodbath. Escapes with Mara into freedom.
    • Chapter 7 – The Chemist and the Mathemagician (Crisis): Kiwi trains Fin and Mara in wilderness survival after their escape. Fin’s mathemagician/classless toolkit begins to matter strategically, showing that his unusual class can do things normal classes cannot.
    • Chapter 12 – McBreakfast Sandwiches (Crisis): Kiwi, Fin, and Mara flee from pursuing mechs and discover an abandoned town. Kiwi buys it through the town ownership interface and renames it McBreakfast Sandwiches, making Mara assistant administrator. The town becomes their refuge. Gelkorus locates and recaptures Kiwi, separating her from Fin and Mara.
    • Chapter 15 – The Hunter and the Hunted (Crisis): Marnie tracks Fin and Mara to McBreakfast Sandwiches. Fin and Mara trap her, kill her, and repeatedly control her respawns via the caged save point – turning the slaving system’s own mechanics against their former oppressor. Fin frames the act as justice and level-recovery rather than revenge.
    • Chapter 23 – Message in a Bottle (Crisis): Kiwi, unable to escape physically, exploits the city administration interface of McBreakfast Sandwiches to send her coordinates out through the town’s systems. Fin and Mara relay the message.
    • Chapter 25 – Beachhead (Crisis): Tasha’s allies arrive and turn McBreakfast Sandwiches into a functional beachhead and refugee node. Fin relays Kiwi’s coordinates to the rescue party, enabling the strike on [Penfold’s lab](./location-mines-of-bahg-taldur.html). He becomes a key intelligence link in the campaign.
    • Epilogue (Crisis): Fin returns home to his village of Elanweer after the war, finally free and reunited with his people.

  • Relationships:
    • Mara: Chosen sibling bond. Not biological siblings, but Mara calls Fin her brother and treats him as family. They escaped slavery together and co-manage McBreakfast Sandwiches. Fin is fiercely protective of her.
    • Princess Kiwistafel: Ally and co-escapee. Kiwi trained Fin and Mara in wilderness survival and trusted them with the administration of McBreakfast Sandwiches. Kiwi told Trista to “protect the kids” (Fin and Mara) rather than attempt an impossible rescue of herself.
    • Marnie: Former enslaver turned prey. Marnie tracked Fin and Mara to McBreakfast Sandwiches; Fin and Mara killed her and controlled her respawns via the caged save point.
    • Pelleas: Fellow enslaved elf and friend from captivity. One of the few people who cheered Fin up during slavery.
    • Cerribor: Protective battlefield ally during the slave-side Crisis arc.
    • Gelkorus: Antagonist who recaptured Kiwi and separated her from Fin and Mara.
