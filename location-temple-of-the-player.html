Temple of the Player

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Locations

A mysterious dungeon that appears around people during thunderstorms. Contains a statue room, small library with an API manual for spell programming, and is guarded by the Master Glove boss. Tasha arrives here first and meets Ari and Pan. Contains a save point. Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos

Tall pedestals line the walls in a wide semicircle, each bearing humanoid statues of different adventurers — men, women, and children frozen in heroic poses. Several pedestals stand empty, their statues shattered, clay fragments scattered at the base. A massive stone door dominates one wall, distinguishable only by a slightly darker shade of stone. Pressure plates and a heavy metal crate sit on the floor near the door. In the weapon room beyond, blades, staves, firearms, and a floating glass orb fill the space. Atmospheric, mysterious, and ancient — the air hums with barely perceptible music that seems to emanate from the walls themselves. A save point glows faintly on a stone platform. Thunder crashes outside, hinting at the storm that summoned this place into being.
