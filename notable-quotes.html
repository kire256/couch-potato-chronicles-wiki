Tasha Singleton — Couch Potato Chaos

Iolo Questgiver — Couch Potato Chaos

So the past has caught up with us at last.

Tempest is my favorite spell, but the casting sequence is elaborate and I need room to move. It’s not practical in confined spaces.

Captain K’her Noálin — Couch Potato Chaos

If I ever had a conscience, I’d have sold it many years ago.

Queen Aralynn Murderjoy — Couch Potato Chaos

Every day, I kill thousands of elven slaves. This is how I’ve gained a higher level than anyone else in Etheria, through the mass blood sacrifice of my enemies.
