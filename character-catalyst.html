Catalyst

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Characters View relationship charts

Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos

Profile

  • Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos
  • Aliases: The Eidolon of Change
  • Role: One of the four eidolons — cosmic entities that govern fundamental forces. Catalyst is the eidolon of change, responsible for drawing the Player from Earth into Etheria and initiating transformative events.
  • Personality: Catalyst is the most approachable and least terrifying of the four eidolons — a being whose domain is change itself, manifested through thundercloud imagery, electrical storms, and the crack of transformation. Where Entropy destroys, Libra reveals, and Snickers deceives, Catalyst invites. The eidolon’s interaction with Tasha is brief but pivotal: an offer extended during a thunderstorm, a choice given rather than forced. This suggests a cosmic entity that respects free will even as it sets world-altering events in motion. Catalyst does not coerce — it presents opportunity. The offer of “adventure” is genuine, though the consequences of accepting ripple far beyond anything Tasha could have imagined. As the force that bridges Earth and Etheria, Catalyst stands at the threshold between worlds — literally the catalyst for every event that follows.
  • Backstory: As one of the four fundamental eidolons alongside Entropy, Libra, and [Snickers the Bumble](./character-snickers-the-bumble.html), Catalyst has existed since before Etheria’s recorded history. The eidolons are not created beings but intrinsic forces — cosmic constants given form and will. Catalyst’s specific domain is change: transformation, new beginnings, the pivotal moment when one state becomes another. The [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html), which materialises during thunderstorms and serves as the gateway between Earth and Etheria, appears to be connected to Catalyst’s influence. When [Jak Singleton](./character-jak-singleton.html) — [the fifth Player](./character-jak-singleton.html) — left behind a game cartridge that eventually reached his daughter Tasha, that artifact became the conduit through which Catalyst made contact. Whether Jak himself interacted with Catalyst during his time in Etheria is likely but unconfirmed. Catalyst appeared to Tasha during a thunderstorm (its signature manifestation) and offered her adventure; her acceptance transported her through the [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html) into Etheria as a level 1 Couch Potato, beginning the entire Couch Potato Chronicles saga.
  • Significant story events:
    • Chapter 1 — Catalyst (Chaos): The eidolon’s defining appearance. During a thunderstorm, Catalyst offers [Tasha Singleton](./character-tasha-singleton.html) — an overworked game developer from Earth — the choice of adventure. She accepts and is transported through the [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html) into Etheria. The chapter that bears Catalyst’s name establishes the entire series’ premise and introduces the thunderstorm imagery that defines the eidolon’s visual identity.
    • Chapter 2 — The Inverted Clock (Chaos): The mysterious countdown appears on clocks in both Earth and Etheria. While Catalyst is not directly named, the countdown’s appearance coincides with the Player’s arrival — suggesting Catalyst’s intervention may have triggered or accelerated the countdown mechanism tied to the Entropy Bargain.
    • Chapter 5 — Denver (Chaos): The [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html), through which Catalyst transported Tasha, continues to be significant as the location where Tasha meets Denver, dies for the first time, and respawns as [Warrior Tasha](./character-tasha-singleton.html). The temple’s materialisation during thunderstorms links it to Catalyst’s domain.
    • References in Tasha’s arc: Tasha’s relationship with Catalyst is described as “transactional at first but carries cosmic weight — Catalyst chose her, and that choice echoes through both books.” The eidolon’s selection of Tasha as the Player is the inciting event of the entire series.

  • Physical description: Catalyst manifests through thundercloud and electrical storm imagery rather than taking a fixed corporeal form. The eidolon is associated with dark, roiling clouds, lightning, and the charged atmosphere of a building storm. When Catalyst appears to Tasha, it is during a thunderstorm — suggesting the eidolon either IS the storm or speaks through it. As a cosmic entity, Catalyst likely has no single “true” form comparable to mortal beings, but its chosen visual language is consistently that of thunder, lightning, and the moment of transformation that a storm represents.
  • Image Generation Prompt: A vast, sentient thunderstorm hovering above a suburban Earth living room at night, seen through a window — the boundary between the mundane and the cosmic. The clouds are impossibly dark and dense, swirling with internal lightning that forms patterns suggesting a face or consciousness within the storm. Bolts of white-blue electricity arc from cloud to cloud, and a single, massive lightning strike descends toward the ground where a figure (Tasha, seen from behind) stands silhouetted against the light. The storm has an unmistakable sense of invitation rather than menace — this is not a destructive weather event but a gateway, a threshold. The clouds part slightly at the centre, revealing a depth that suggests another world beyond (a faint, warm golden glow — the first hint of Etheria). Rain falls in curtains around the scene. The overall palette is deep indigo, charcoal, and electric blue-white, with the warm golden glow of Etheria providing the only contrast. Style: cinematic fantasy illustration, dramatic lighting, highly atmospheric. The mood is awe, possibility, and the electric moment before everything changes.
  • Relationships:
    • [Tasha Singleton](./character-tasha-singleton.html) (chosen Player): The defining mortal-eidolon relationship of the series. Catalyst selected Tasha — or perhaps her father Jak — as the Player who would enter Etheria. Tasha’s relationship with Catalyst is “transactional at first but carries cosmic weight — Catalyst chose her, and that choice echoes through both books.” The eidolon’s offer of adventure, delivered during a thunderstorm, sets every subsequent event in motion.
    • [Jak Singleton](./character-jak-singleton.html) (prior Player, deceased): Jak’s game cartridge served as the conduit through which Catalyst contacted Tasha. Whether Jak himself interacted with Catalyst during his time as [the fifth Player](./character-jak-singleton.html) is likely but unconfirmed. His legacy is the bridge between Catalyst’s offer and Tasha’s acceptance.
    • Entropy, Libra, [Snickers the Bumble](./character-snickers-the-bumble.html) (fellow eidolons): The four cosmic entities form a system of fundamental forces — change, destruction, information, and mischief. Catalyst’s domain of transformation stands in direct opposition to Entropy’s domain of annihilation, while Snickers serves as mediator and Libra as oracle.
    • The [Temple of the Player](./location-temple-of-the-player.html) (associated location): The temple that materialises during thunderstorms — Catalyst’s signature weather — and serves as the gateway between Earth and Etheria. Catalyst’s influence is felt here most strongly, as it is the place where Players arrive and where transformation begins.
