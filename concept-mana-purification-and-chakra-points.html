Mana purification and chakra points

Source: wiki.md

← Back to Concepts & Lore

Mana has varying levels of quality independent of stat points. Dark elves traditionally channel mana through chakra points to purify it as it leaves the body, amplifying spell effects. The technique is taught to dark-elven children from a young age. Kiwi struggles with this technique because she was trained by a dark-elven magus despite being a high elf. Appears in: Couch Potato Chaos
